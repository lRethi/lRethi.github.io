Cronofobia

[~/projects] >>> cat cronofobia.info

Cronofobia is a narrative survival game set in a dystopian version of Brazil in 204X. The player takes control of Geraldo Salvador, a person experiencing homelessness who becomes subject to ULTRALINK, a biomechanical artificial intelligence implant.

Across a seven-day countdown to Christmas Eve, the player explores an urban environment, talks to its inhabitants, completes quests, and manages limited resources. Choices can affect relationships, mission outcomes, and the wider course of the story.

The gameplay combines time progression, hunger and thirst management, a limited inventory, item collection, purchases and theft, character quests, branching conversations, and enemy pursuit. Persistent game-state flags connect player actions to later events and narrative consequences.

ULTRALINK-related modifications can further alter the player's experience, reflecting the growing influence and instability of the implant.
